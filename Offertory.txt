 

Offertory section  

 

1. 

Abba, Father, let us be yours and yours alone, 

Set our hearts our spirits free, 

Make us, Lord, your own. 

Through your goodness, Lord, we bring, 

Bread of our labour and wine to cheer the heart, 

Which will soon be filled for us with the life of your son. 

Abba, Father, let us be yours and yours alone, 

Set our hearts our spirits free, 

Make us, Lord, your own. 

Take this drop of water too, 

Sign of our weakness, Yet symbol of our love, 

By its mingling may we share, Your divinity. 

Abba, Father, let us be yours and yours alone, 

Set our hearts our spirits free, 

Make us, Lord, your own. 

Please, accept our sacrifice, 

Offered to honour the glory of your name. 

Sinners all, we bring to you 

Humble, contrite hearts. 

Abba, Father, let us be yours and yours alone, 

Set our hearts our spirits free, 

Make us, Lord, your own. 

From the rising of the sun, 

Unto its setting, how great is your name. 

All the nations bring you, Lord, 

Fragrant sacrifice. 

 

 

2. 

Blest are You, Lord God of all creation 

Thanks to Your goodness, this bread we offer: 

Fruit of the earth, work of our hands, 

It will become the bread of life. 

Blessed be God, blessed be God, 

Blessed be God, forever. Amen. (x2) 

Blest are you, Lord God of all creation 

Thanks to Your goodness, this wine we offer: 

Fruit of the vine, work of our hands, 

It will become our cup of joy.

4. 

In this Sacred Mystery 

In this sacred mystery 

We praise the Lord of history 

At this Eucharistic feast 

We are all priests. 

O take this bread and take this wine, 

And take our hearts and take our minds. 

At this Eucharistic feast, 

We are all priests. 

In your light and in your sight, 

We celebrate this holy rite. 

As these gifts we offer now, 

Become our light. 

This host of wheat, this cup of wine, 

And all our lives become divine. 

All these gifts we offer now, 

Become our light. 

It is your death we celebrate, 

As our lives we consecrate 

In your strength our sins forgiven, 

Our love renewed. 

We come in sin, it is our shame, 

You are our hope we call your name, 

In your strength our sins forgiven, 

Our love renewed. 

 

 

5. 

Lord, accept this bread and wine; 

Take our hearts and make them thine, 

Take our work and our anxiety, 

Give us life and liberty. 

Change this bread into your body 

And this wine into your blood; 

Change our lives, make us united, Lord, 

To spread the love of Christ, the Lord. 

 

 

6. 

Lift up your hands and offer to the Lord, 

Lift up your eyes and see the works of  God, 

Lift up your heart unto the heart of God, 

Offer your love with his love to accord. 

Through your goodness, Lord, we bring, gifts of  Bread and wine, 

Which will soon become for us, life-giving food and drink. 

Lift up your hands and offer to the Lord, 

Lift up your eyes and see the works of  God, 

Lift up your heart unto the heart of God, 

Offer your love with his love to accord. 

Take this drop of water too, our humanity, 

By its mingling make us share, in your divinity. 

Lift up your hands and offer to the Lord, 

Lift up your eyes and see the works of  God, 

Lift up your heart unto the heart of God, 

Offer your love with his love to accord. 

Lord, we ask you, please accept, this, our sacrifice, 

Sinners all, we bring to you, humble and contrite hearts.
